01 / ABOUT

A foundation in theory.
A focus on networks.

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Electronic Engineering at Shantou University. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from City University of Hong Kong in 2021, supervised by Prof. Lin Dai, and my bachelor's and master's degrees from South China University of Technology.

My research interests include cell-free massive MIMO, network deployment, stochastic optimization, and machine learning for wireless communications. I also work on pinching-antenna systems, wireless content caching, and mobile crowdsensing.
